The app for the book “Healing as Remembering”

Understanding Healing

Deeper than the masks.

Beneath the surface of everyday life, patterns are at work that we rarely notice: protective strategies formed early, which keep running unconsciously and hold us in a fixation. The Enneagram describes nine such basic movements, each with its own wound and longing. The app explains, in short questions and answers, how fixation arises, what shadow and ego mean and which way back is meant. On your phone, in eight languages: German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese.

What the app offers

The nine paths

An Enneagram figure on the start page: tap a number and read about the wound, the movement and the longing of that type, and about the way back. Each number carries the colour of its type.

Questions and answers

93 answers on healing, balance, shadow, ego, fixation and projection and on the nine types, many with an expandable deeper part, arranged in topic groups.

Awareness practices

25 practices, each with an opening picture: pausing, breath, movement, observing, habits. Not a treatment, only suggestions to try.

Index and search

Keywords for looking things up and a search across all entries.

Share as a picture card

Every question and every practice can be passed on as a picture card with the answer, the app name and a link.

A song for the app

“Deeper Than the Masks” by Detlef Rathmer, to listen to in the app. The German version is called “Tiefer als die Masken”.

Eight languages

German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese. You switch with one tap; questions, practices and index are translated as well.

Book and app

Two versions, each made for a different purpose.

The book

“Heilung als Erinnerung” (German edition) is made for reading and leads through all chapters in context.

The app

Condenses the key ideas into short entries, with practices, search and picture cards to pass on. If you want to go deeper, read the book.

The book behind the app

“Heilung als Erinnerung – Der Weg zurück zur Einheit durch Bewusstsein, Integration und das Enneagramm” (German edition) was published by Verlagshaus Rathmer. The app makes its key ideas usable on your phone.
