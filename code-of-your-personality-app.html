What the app offers

Your investigation file

A detective as your companion. If you like, put your own photo on the investigator ID. The photo stays on your device only.

Clue search

Center, energy pole, type and subtype: step by step, with the descriptions from the book.

Case file with five clues

Center, energy pole, quality of gaze, life story and subtype. You secure each clue with a note of your own. The notes stay on your device.

The whole book to look things up

All chapters with search, bookmarks and read-aloud. Works offline too.

A song for the book

“Footsteps through Velvet Streets” by Detlef Rathmer, quietly in the background, across all tabs.

“The Code of Your Personality” is available as a paperback and as an e-book from BoD. The app makes the content usable on your phone: with search, read-aloud, bookmarks and tools for everyday life.
