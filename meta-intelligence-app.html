What the app offers

Observation practices to try yourself

The practices from the eleven chapters: read the text, start the timer, then note what you noticed. Your notes stay on your device.

Silence timer

3 to 20 minutes, with a singing bowl at the start and end. A moment without thinking about what comes next.

The whole book to look things up

All chapters with search, bookmarks and read-aloud. Works offline too.

Thought and question of the day

A thought from the book and a question that invites you to pause. Plus the weather, as in the other apps.

A song for the book

“Reflections in a Gentle Breeze” by Detlef Rathmer, quietly in the background, across all tabs.
