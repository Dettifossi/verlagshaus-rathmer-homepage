What the app audiobook offers

Twenty-seven people

Each of the nine Enneagram types in three instinct variants, each variant a person of their own with a story of their own, each with a portrait.

To listen to

About ten minutes per story, in the author’s voice (digitally voiced, with the author’s consent). Speed from 0.85 to 1.25, skips of 15 seconds.

To read along

The full text of every story. It follows along while you listen, and you can switch between listening and reading at any time.

Sleep timer

A story to fall asleep to: the timer stops after 10, 20 or 30 minutes, or at the end of the story.

A book that opens spaces

No quick answers, but questions, resonance and the quiet certainty that life carries, even in the depths.

The book behind the app

“In der Tiefe der Zeit” tells of people at moments when life holds its breath: farewell, illness, an unexpected break. Inspired by the basic patterns of the Enneagram. The book is available as a paperback (€15.95) from BoD (German edition).
