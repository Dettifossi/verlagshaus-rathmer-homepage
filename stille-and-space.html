Frequently asked questions

What does the app cost?
The trial version is free. The full version with all sounds, exercises and paths is available in the app: one-time 19.99 €, no subscription.
Do I need to know the Enneagram?
No. The type is optional and the app works entirely without that knowledge. If you would like to find your type, the app helps you with that.
What about privacy?
No account, no tracking. Journal, favorites and type stay on your device. Videos only load when you tap ▶.
Which devices does it run on?
In the browser on iPhone, iPad, Android and computer. Added to the home screen it opens like an app. The interface is available in German and English.
