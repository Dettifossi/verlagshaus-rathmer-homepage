What the app offers

All 27 subtypes

Three subtypes per type (self-preservation, social, sexual), each in seven sections, with keywords for its expression.

The nine types

The basics of the nine types from the book, in the colours of the types.

Recognising

Quality of gaze and body language, mistypings and lookalikes, and practical application in everyday life.

The whole book

All chapters and the glossary to look things up, with search across the full text, read-aloud, bookmarks and song. Subtype of the day, everything shareable as a card.

The book behind the app

“The Hidden Dynamics of the 27 Subtypes” is available as a paperback (€24.90) on Amazon, and in German as a paperback and e-book from BoD. The app makes the content usable on your phone: with search, read-aloud, bookmarks and quick access to each of the 27 subtypes.
