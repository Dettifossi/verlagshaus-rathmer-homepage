The Hidden Dynamics of the 27 Subtypes

The Hidden Dynamics of the 27 Subtypes offers a deeper approach to the Enneagram and demonstrates how the nine core types unfold into 27 differentiated subtypes through the three instinctual orientations. The book combines foundational knowledge with a precise exploration of the motivations, perceptions, observational qualities, body language, development, and maturation of the subtypes. It reveals that personality can be understood not only conceptually, but also through expression, interpersonal contact, and its inner dynamics. A comprehensive foundational work for anyone who wants to understand the Enneagram in greater depth and from a new perspective.
