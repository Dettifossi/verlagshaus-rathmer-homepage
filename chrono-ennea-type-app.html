Chrono Ennea Type

Eating in rhythm with the organs: according to the Chinese organ clock and the Enneagram.

The book by Detlef Rathmer as an app: the twelve organ times, your Enneagram type and subtype, plus recipes, seasons and mindfulness practices. On your phone, always at hand.

What the app offers

The organ clock

Twelve organs, twelve time windows, colored like the book cover. The current window lights up; each organ opens its section from the book.

My type

Choose type and subtype, or only the type. The start page shows the suggestion for the time of day that fits you.

27 subtypes with a practice

Each subtype with nutrition recommendations, link to the organ clock and a mindfulness practice in three steps.

Recipes and seasons

Recipes from the book by organ time, plus the five seasons and what is on right now.

The song for the app

“Between the Clocks and the Sky” by Detlef Rathmer, with volume and pause.

Share and read aloud

Share suggestions as an image card, have chapters read aloud, usable offline.

German and English

One tap on “EN” or “DE” at the top right switches the language.
