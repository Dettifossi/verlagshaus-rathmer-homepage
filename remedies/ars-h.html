Arsenicum hydrogenisatum (Ars‑h.)

Arsenicum hydrogenisatum (Ars‑h.) appears in 74 rubrics in the Repertorium. Of these, 6 are single rubrics in which only this remedy is listed, and 4 are small rubrics with at most two other remedies. The emphasis is on Fear and Anxiety, and also on Aversion to work and occupation. The data are slim, and the picture is correspondingly outlined.

Nocturnal fear of disease

Fear stands in the foreground and has a fixed time. It concerns impending disease and occurs at night in bed, especially on waking: "Fear – disease, impending – night in bed – waking, on". The associated anxiety about diseases on waking at night and an anxiety about frost belong in the same context. Among all the rubrics of the remedy, Fear, Anxiety and Death are the most numerous.

Turning away from work

Alongside stands a turning away directed at working life: disgust for work in the occupation and for one's own business ("Disgust, general – business, for his/her"). Indifference to important news and impaired mental activity fit in.

Peculiarities close to the body

Two single rubrics connect mind and body: "Despair – vomiting, during" and "Delusions – head – swimming".

Summary

The few specific rubrics show a person whom concern for his own health overtakes at night and who turns away from his work and his business by day.

All rubrics of this remedy, the materia medica and the case work are in Rathmer’s Repertory.
