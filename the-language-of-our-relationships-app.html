What the app offers

Type pairs and subtype pairs

Choose two types and see where they understand each other and what triggers them, with everyday examples and a practice for both.

Save several relationships

Partner, child, parents, friends, colleagues: save a name and role and switch between relationships with one tap. The names stay on your device.

For both of us

Send a practice or the question of the day to the other person with one tap: “Would you like to try this with me?”

The whole book

All chapters, the relationship lexicon and the glossary to look things up, with search, read-aloud and bookmarks.
