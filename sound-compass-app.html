The music app

Sound Compass

All songs by Detlef Rathmer, in one place.

For two decades Detlef Rathmer has also expressed the themes of his life's work in music, alongside his therapeutic practice, his books, apps and calendars. Sound Compass brings these songs together: 1,425 pieces with about 84 hours of music that you can find by type, mood, style and album. Plus a sphere that pulses with the beat of the song.

What the app offers

1,425 songs, 84 hours

All songs by Detlef Rathmer in one place: Enneagram, jazz, spiritual songs, love songs, humour and many styles.

By type and instinct

Type 1 to 9, plus the instincts SP, SO and SX. Every type has its colour. Songs for the whole type have their own place.

By mood and tempo

Calm, gentle, thoughtful, cheerful or powerful. Mixes such as “To fall asleep”, “To dance” or “Instrumental only”.

Styles and categories

Jazz, Irish, bossa nova, reggae, classical, rap, Comedian Harmonists style and more. Categories such as “Love songs by Detlef” and “Spiritual songs”.

Albums

More than 50 albums, ordered by number, from the genre albums and healing music to the jazz albums.

The sphere

Globe, disco ball or waves: the visuals pulse with the beat of the song. Seven colour themes to choose from.

With vocals or instrumental

A filter separates songs with vocals from pure music, for reading, working or falling asleep.

Favourites, sleep timer, sharing

Save songs, set a sleep timer and share a song by link.

Languages

The app is available in eleven languages: German, English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Hindi, Ukrainian and Chinese. The songs are sung in eleven languages as well: 🇬🇧 🇩🇪 🇫🇷 🇧🇷 🇷🇺 🇮🇳 🇨🇳 🇪🇸 🇮🇹 🇹🇷 🇺🇦
Most are in English and German, plus songs in French, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Hindi, Chinese, Spanish, Italian, Turkish and Ukrainian. A filter in the app shows the songs by language.
