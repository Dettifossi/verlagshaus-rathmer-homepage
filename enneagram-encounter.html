What you will find in the app

Relationship evaluation

People are often mistyped in the Enneagram. The seal shows who has been typed personally by Detlef Rathmer or David L. Rathmer. It is voluntary. Anyone who types themselves is equally welcome.

Frequently asked questions

What does the app cost?
Nothing. We charge no membership fee.
Do I need to know the Enneagram?
No. The evaluation explains itself, and the library helps you get started.
Do I have to be typed?
No. The Type Seal is a voluntary offer for those who would like to be typed personally, for example by David L. Rathmer or Detlef Rathmer.
What happens to my data?
The current app stores nothing about you on a server. Your settings stay on your device. Details are in the privacy policy in the app under “More”.
Are there profiles and encounters yet?
The evaluation, library, music and impulses are available now. Profiles and the actual encounter will follow later.

Note: The evaluations are suggestions, not a diagnosis and not a substitute for medical, psychotherapeutic or medical advice. A Type Seal is a professional assessment without guarantee.
